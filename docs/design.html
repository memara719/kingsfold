Kingsfold

Build a village. Grow a kingdom. First to 8 crowns wins.

  • 3–6 PLAYERS
  • 30–40 MIN
  • ONLINE
KING ALDRIC SAYS:

Welcome to Kingsfold! Every village you build makes my kingdom grow, and every one earns you a crown. Get 8 crowns and I'll name you heir to the throne.

YOUR TURN

  1. 1. ROLL

    Every land showing that number pays its neighbors. Everyone collects, even on your turn.

  2. 2. TRADE

    Swap cards with friends, or give the bank 4 of one kind for 1 of anything.

  3. 3. BUILD

    Spend cards. The spots you can build on glow. Tap one.

That's the whole game.

WHAT YOU CAN BUILD

WORKSHOPS

Add one to any village or town. Each costs 2 cards and makes that place better.

HOW TO WIN

    THE BANDITS

    They hide in the slum behind the castle. Roll a 7 and they come out. Put them on any land: it stops paying, and you steal 1 card from a player living there.

    Anyone holding more than 7 cards loses half of them. Play a Knight to chase the bandits somewhere else.

    PICK A HOUSE

    Each player picks a noble house before the game. One small power each, easy to remember.

    NEWS FROM THE KING

    Each new round, the King announces one bit of news. It lasts one round and keeps every game a little different.

    HARVEST FESTIVAL

    Fields pay double this round.

    THE QUEST BOARD

    Three quests are always pinned up. Finish one first and it's worth a crown. Then a new quest goes up.

    NO RULEBOOK NEEDED

    New players should be able to learn by playing. The screen teaches them:

    • Only moves you can make light up. Tap a glowing spot to build there.
    • Every button shows its price. If you can't afford something, it's grayed out and shows what you're missing.
    • Tap any land to see what it pays and who collects.
    • In your first game, the King pops up with a short tip right when you need it.
    • Changed your mind? Undo a build any time before you end your turn.
    • Turns have a 90-second sand timer, so nobody waits long.

    PLAYING ONLINE

    Open it in your browser, on a computer or a phone. One friend makes a room and shares a 4-letter code, and everyone else types it in. If your connection drops, you rejoin right where you left off. The music is old-school chiptune.
